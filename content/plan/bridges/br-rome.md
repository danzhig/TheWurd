Simon's heirs, the Hasmoneans, ruled an independent Jewish state for about eighty years. In this era the Pharisees and Sadducees took shape, synagogues spread, and the community behind the Dead Sea Scrolls withdrew to the desert. Rome's general Pompey took Jerusalem in 63 BC. Rome later made Herod king of Judea; he rebuilt the temple on a grand scale. Luke opens in Herod's last years.
