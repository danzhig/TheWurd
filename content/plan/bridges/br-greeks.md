Nehemiah ends around 430 BC in a small Persian province. A century later Alexander the Great conquered Persia, and Judea passed to his successors: first the Ptolemies of Egypt, then, from about 200 BC, the Seleucids of Syria. In Alexandria, Jewish scholars translated the Scriptures into Greek, the Septuagint, which the early church would read. 1 Maccabees opens with Alexander and moves quickly to the crisis of 167 BC.
