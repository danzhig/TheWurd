## How it is divided

- **Chapters 1–7, the offerings**, are split three ways so no section runs past about ten minutes: the burnt, grain and peace offerings (1–3); the sin and guilt offerings (4:1–5:26); then the instructions to the priests for each offering (6–7). In the Hebrew numbering the guilt offering ends at 5:26; WEBC prints those verses as 6:1–7, so the WEBC column shows 4:1–6:7.
- **Chapters 8–10** keep the ordination (8–9) apart from the death of Nadab and Abihu (10), the book's one story.
- **Chapters 11–15**, the laws of clean and unclean, follow their own topics: food (11), childbirth and skin disease (12–13), then cleansing, mould and discharges (14–15).
- **Chapter 16**, the Day of Atonement, stands alone.
- **Chapters 17–26** go a chapter at a time where a chapter has one subject, and 20–22 (penalties, then the holiness of priests and offerings) together.
- **Chapter 27**, on vows and tithes, closes the book.

Each chapter's subject was checked against the WEBC text by counting its key words (for example _atonement_ in 16, _jubilee_ and _redeem_ in 25, _redeem_ and _tithe_ in 27); see `Lev-outline.md` for the chapter outline and the verses the plan numbers differently.
